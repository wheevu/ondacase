# Case 001 authoring record

This file contains spoilers and exists for logic and narrative development.

## What happened

Eli Venn planned to publish proof that food columnist Arin Ko sold favorable reviews.
At 19:04, Arin bought aconite tincture from the pharmacy counter inside the night store and called it cough drops when asked.
At 19:20, during a recurring camera fault, Arin lifted Eli's iced americano from the pickup shelf, put aconite beneath the lid, wiped the rim, and returned the cup.
Eli drank at 19:24 and died between 19:25 and 19:28.
Arin left through the service alley at 19:25.

Mira Vale is the strongest early alternative.
She said she left at 19:10, but Receipt #004 records her in-person iced americano purchase at 19:22.
She lied to hide an affair with Eli, not the murder.
After the receipt confrontation, she admits that she saw Arin beside Eli's cup.

## People and deception

- Mira, café manager. Public goal: protect the café. Private goal: keep her affair with Eli private. Her 19:10 departure is a direct falsehood. She folds till paper into tiny birds when anxious.
- Arin, food columnist. Public goal: frame the poison purchase as research. Private goal: stop Eli's review-fraud story. His cough-drop claim is directly false. He peels labels from bottles while talking.
- Jo, barista. Public goal: keep her job. Private goal: hide that she opened the camera panel. Her account of a familiar person with the cup is incomplete, and her identification of Mira is mistaken. She is studying municipal horticulture at night.
- Sasha, Eli's former partner. Public goal: recover her key. Private goal: hide debt and an argument about it. "I never raised my voice to him" is technically true and meant to imply there was no argument. She repairs old radios.
- Dan, delivery rider. Public goal: finish his route and leave. Private goal: hide the stolen parcel. "I never went inside the café" is technically true but implies he was nowhere near the service door. His route recollection is also mistaken. He keeps condiment sachets for his grandmother.

## Evidence

- `receipt_004`: Mira's 19:22 in-person iced americano purchase. It disproves her departure claim but does not imply guilt.
- `camera_log`: outage from 19:18 through 19:29. It creates shared opportunity.
- `toxicology`: aconite in the drink and a 19:25-19:28 death window.
- `pharmacy_footage`: Arin buying aconite at 19:04. It establishes means and disproves his cough-drop claim.
- `cup_lid`: aconite under a wiped rim. It establishes delivery method and attempted concealment.
- `tape_fiber`: blue adhesive fiber beneath the lid matches the tape on Arin's cut index finger. Before `cup_lid`, it looks like ordinary lint. Together they establish contact with the concealed surface.
- `draft_email`: Eli's scheduled article and supporting invoices establish Arin's motive.
- `service_log`: Arin's staff-loaned access tag opens the service door at 19:17 and exits at 19:25.
- `mira_statement`: unlocked by the receipt confrontation; she saw Arin beside Eli's booth at 19:23.
- `delivery_photo`: places Dan and the stolen parcel outside at 19:19, then his tracker places him two blocks away by 19:24.
- `sasha_voicemail`: places Sasha on a live call outside from 19:20 through 19:27.
- `panel_log`: Jo opened the camera panel at 19:16 but remained signed into the till through 19:24.

## Proof threshold

The minimum complete case needs `toxicology`, `pharmacy_footage`, `cup_lid`, `tape_fiber`, `draft_email`, and `service_log`.
These establish method, means, physical contact, motive, and opportunity.
At least three exclusion records among `delivery_photo`, `sasha_voicemail`, `panel_log`, and the receipt-unlocked `mira_statement` are also required to eliminate alternatives.

Before the lid and fiber are connected, Arin is only one plausible suspect.
Before enough exclusions are found, declining to accuse is a rigorous outcome.
The full set uniquely establishes Arin.

## Alternative theories

- Mira fits access, opportunity, a false timeline, and personal motive. She lacks poison and physical contact. Her later statement plus the matching fiber eliminate her.
- Sasha fits debt, anger, and key access. The live voicemail excludes her from the death window, and no evidence links her to aconite.
- Jo caused the camera gap and handled café cups. Till activity and the tape fiber exclude her.
- Dan was at the service door and lied about why. His photo and tracker reveal theft but place him away before Eli drinks.

## Ending rules

- Conviction: correct suspect, coherent method and opportunity, all required proof dimensions, and no viable alternative.
- Lucky idiot: Arin accused without a complete or unique proof.
- Beautiful theory: another suspect accused with a coherent but contradicted sequence.
- The Detective: the player closes the file while the visible evidence genuinely leaves alternatives.
- Everybody goes home: the player closes the file despite possessing a unique complete proof.
