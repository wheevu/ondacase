## Risk areas and review focus

- Medium risk: shared controls, focus order, navigation, and player-knowledge diagrams.
  Review those areas as one completed deliverable, including small windows and pagination.
- The map is a static 3D render, not a real-time navigable 3D scene.
  Its arrangement is explicitly schematic and must not be treated as proof of distance, adjacency, or travel time.
- Map anchors are projected by the Blender script and stored in the renderer.
  A camera change requires updating those anchors and rechecking all location overlays.
- The title, map, and location previews now use the original district art.
  Old scene artwork remains on disk with its previous unresolved provenance warning; this change does not grant redistribution rights to those files.
- Blender 5.2 emits the known `Material.use_nodes` deprecation warning for Blender 6.0, but the documented export succeeds.
- The full-check fixture issue remains outside this UI task.
- No independent review, commit, push, deployment, or external state change was performed.
